three steps

Build it once. Walk away.

1

Pick a trigger.

A form. A webhook. An email landing in your inbox. Every Monday at 9.

2

Add steps that think.

Read this. Decide that. Write the reply. Skip the ones that don't matter.

3

Press run and go to bed.

Every step shows what went in, what came out, how long it took, and what it cost. If something breaks, you'll know which step and why — not just "Workflow failed."

what's inside

Build it. Run it. Hand it off.

One product, three stages. Here's what you get at each one.

1

Build it

Draw it, don't code it.

  • 🧱

    A canvas of steps

    Drag triggers, logic and AI steps onto a grid. 60+ apps are built in.

  • 📚

    Knowledge

    Give it your docs and files, and it answers from them.

  • 🧩

    Skills and templates

    Save a step once and reuse it, or start from a ready-made template.

2

Run it

It works while you don't.

  • ⏰

    Starts on its own

    A schedule, a webhook, an incoming email or an event in one of your apps.

  • 🤖

    Agents that act

    Chat with an agent that uses your tools and runs your workflows. It asks before anything important.

  • 💬

    An assistant that's awake

    A morning briefing, a self-sorting inbox and meeting prep, in Slack, email, SMS or the web.

3

Hand it off

Share it, trust it.

  • 🔗

    Pages, apps and an API

    Turn a workflow into a page or small app for clients, or call it from your own code.

  • 👥

    Built for a team

    Shared workspaces, members and roles, so everyone sees only what they should.

  • 🛡️

    Approvals, audit trail, vault

    A human signs off when it matters, every run is recorded, and your keys stay locked away.
