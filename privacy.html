2. What we collect

When you join the waitlist. Your email address. Nothing else. If you answer our question about the automation you gave up on, we keep your answer.

3. The data you put through your workflows

This is the section worth reading properly.

Your workflows will process data that isn't yours. Your customers' names. Their emails. Whatever a form submission contains. That data passes through our systems, gets sent to AI models, and gets stored in your run history.

In legal terms: for that data, you are the controller and we are the processor. You decide what goes in. We only do what your workflow instructs. We don't use it for anything else — not to improve our product, not to train anything, not for analytics.

In practical terms: don't put data through Agent1o1 that you aren't allowed to send to a third party. If you're processing personal data of people in Europe, you need a lawful basis for it, and you need a Data Processing Agreement with us. Ask and we'll sign one.

Run history contains that data. If a node processed someone's email address, that email address is sitting in the run log. You can delete individual runs at any time, and you can set your workspace to keep run history for [N] days and no longer.

4. AI model providers

When a workflow reaches an AI node, the contents of that node — your prompt, plus whatever data your workflow fed into it — are sent to a model provider to be processed.

Which provider depends on which model you chose. Current providers are listed in section 7.

If you bring your own API key, your data goes directly to that provider under your agreement with them, not ours. Their terms govern what they do with it. We pass it through and store the result in your run history.

If you use our credits, we send your data under our agreement with the provider. We use accounts configured so that your data is not used to train their models. We can't make promises on their behalf beyond what our contract with them says, so if this matters to you, bring your own key and read their terms yourself.

5. Your API keys

If you bring your own key, we store it encrypted at rest. It is decrypted only at the moment a workflow needs it, and it is never written to logs, never shown back to you in full, and never sent anywhere except the provider it belongs to.

Nobody at Agent1o1 can read your keys. If you delete a key, it's gone and we can't recover it.

10. What you can ask us to do

11. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest. API keys separately encrypted. Access to production limited to [N] person. Two-factor authentication on every system we can put it on. Automated backups, tested restores.

We're a small operation. We've built this carefully, but no system is perfect. If you find a vulnerability, email security@agent1o1.com and we'll respond within a day. We won't take legal action against anyone who reports a problem in good faith.

If we ever suffer a breach that puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the relevant authority, promptly and in plain language, including what we don't yet know.
