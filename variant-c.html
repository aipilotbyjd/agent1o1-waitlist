Three steps

Build it once. Walk away.

1

Pick a trigger.

A form. A webhook. An email landing in your inbox. Every Monday at 9.

2

Add steps that think.

Read this. Decide that. Write the reply. Skip the ones that don't matter.

3

Press run and go to bed.

Every step shows what went in, what came out, how long it took, and what it cost. If something breaks, you'll know which step and why — not just "Workflow failed."
